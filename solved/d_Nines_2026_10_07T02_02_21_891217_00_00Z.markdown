State the downtime per year that 99.9% availability allows.

REQUIRED: the yearly downtime to within a few minutes.

## Answer

The downtime per year in minutes can be computed with: (1 - 0.999) . (360 . 24 . 60)

```
(1 - 0.999) * (360 * 24 * 60)
```
