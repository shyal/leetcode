REFERENCE: d231 Powers Of Two
SOURCE: System Design Primer, "Powers of two table" https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer#powers-of-two-table

REQUIRED
- 2^10 = 1024, about 1 thousand, 1 KB.
- 2^20 = 1,048,576, about 1 million, 1 MB.
- 2^30 = 1,073,741,824, about 1 billion, 1 GB.
- 2^40 = 1,099,511,627,776, about 1 trillion, 1 TB.
- 2^8 = 256.
- 2^16 = 65,536, 64 KB.
- 2^32 = 4,294,967,296, 4 GB.

ALSO TRUE
- 2^7 = 128.
